02 — THE PILOT

A STORY
IN PROGRESS ✳

Not a story about having every answer. A story about learning how to ask better questions.

It started with a person, not a product.

A friend once described how lonely it could feel when nobody was immediately available to talk. I wanted to support her. Then a harder question followed: could technology help someone feel heard without pretending to replace a human being?

That question became the starting point for Bloom. As I worked on it, the goal stopped being “make the app reply” and became “understand what a reply might mean, where it could fail, and when it should point someone toward human support.”

That shift now shapes the rest of my work. In TruthTrace, it becomes a question about evidence. In NagarTwin, it becomes a question about assumptions and public decisions. In my smaller apps, it becomes a question about whether a useful feature is dependable outside a demo.

“A working feature is a milestone. Understanding its limits is part of finishing the job.”
