01 / System logic

Simulate. Compare.
Explain the trade-off.

NagarTwin is designed as a transparent decision-support tool, not an oracle. It estimates how a hypothetical event could change a neighbourhood's exposure and access to essential services. You can compare a no-action case against selected interventions and inspect the assumptions behind each score.

STEP 01

Define an event

Select heatwave, rainfall, or power outage and choose a controlled intensity value. This controls severity in the demo model; it is not tied to a real forecast.

STEP 02

Model local impact

Each synthetic zone has a population, vulnerability factor, baseline access score, essential-service counts, and scenario-specific exposure factors.

STEP 03

Test interventions

Intervention toggles change explicit coefficients. Results are repeatable, inspectable, and exportable so you can compare scenarios and reproduce a run.

02 / Trust & limitations

Show the assumptions.
Never fake certainty.

The current version intentionally uses fictional data and simple deterministic rules. It demonstrates the interface and a reproducible scenario workflow; it does not validate a real city's risk, forecast an emergency, or establish that any intervention will work in practice.

What the model uses

  • 12 fictional zones with illustrative population estimates.
  • Scenario-specific baseline exposure factors (heat, rainfall, outage).
  • Vulnerability and essential-service availability indicators.
  • Event-intensity multiplier and explicit intervention deductions.
  • A composite 0–100 access score for comparison across runs.
  • Deterministic logic: same inputs yield the same outputs.

What it does not do

  • It does not use live weather, power-grid, road, clinic, or government feeds.
  • It does not calculate a real flood boundary or engineering-grade heat model.
  • It does not verify submitted community reports or use actual geospatial coordinates.
  • It does not provide emergency directions or guarantee a route is safe.
  • It does not prove causality, fairness, or predictive accuracy.
  • Real deployment needs validated local data, domain experts, and field evaluation.
Safety notice: NagarTwin is a research prototype for scenario exploration. Do not use its fictional outputs to make real emergency, medical, infrastructure, or evacuation decisions. Follow official local authorities and emergency services for live incidents.
